Essential London Travel Tips

  • Getting around: Tap a contactless card or mobile device on the Tube, buses, and Elizabeth line.
  • Museums: Many major national museums offer free general admission—check timed entry slots ahead.
  • Walking & parks: Pair nearby sights along the Thames or across central parks to save transit time.
